# Meeting Research Notes

Create an accurate working record that distinguishes what participants said, what was decided, and what still requires evidence.

## Establish the record

1. Identify meeting purpose, date, participants, source type, and whether speaker labels are reliable.
2. If date, participant, owner, deadline, or decision status is unknown, mark it unknown rather than infer it.
3. Ask before saving durable files. If authorized, copy [`assets/meeting-note-template.md`](assets/meeting-note-template.md) to the user's chosen location.

## Extract in passes

1. **Topics:** Capture the agenda and major discussion threads.
2. **Decisions:** Record explicit decisions, decision owner, date, and any stated rationale.
3. **Commitments:** Capture action, owner, deadline, dependency, and status only when supported by the transcript.
4. **Claims:** Separate evidence-backed facts from participant assertions and assumptions.
5. **Research follow-up:** Convert material unverified claims into answerable research questions and suggest an appropriate source type.
6. **Disagreement:** Preserve objections, alternatives, and unresolved choices.

## Attribution rules

- Attribute a claim only when speaker identity is clear.
- Use `Speaker unknown` when diarization or transcript labels are unreliable.
- Do not infer agreement from silence or attendance.
- Quote only short fragments needed to resolve ambiguity.
- Treat a meeting statement as evidence of what was said, not proof that the statement is true.

## Completion checks

- Match every action owner and deadline to the source.
- Distinguish decisions from proposals and next steps from suggestions.
- Include evidence-to-verify and open-research sections when relevant.
- Minimize confidential and personal information in the output.
- Report the path of any file created or changed.
