# Citation verification protocol

Verify every reference before finalizing academic text.

## Identity check

- Confirm title, author or organization, year, publication venue, and persistent identifier or stable URL.
- Open the source rather than trusting a search snippet or another paper's reference list.
- Confirm that the cited version is the intended version: preprint, accepted manuscript, published article, correction, or retraction.

## Claim check

- Locate the passage, table, figure, dataset, or result supporting the claim.
- Ensure the source supports the exact strength, population, period, and direction stated.
- Record page, section, table, or figure when the citation style or review process requires it.
- Cite primary research for original findings and reviews for field-level synthesis.

## Status check

- Check for corrections, expressions of concern, retractions, and materially superseding versions.
- Distinguish peer-reviewed publications from preprints and working papers.
- Do not infer quality from venue name alone; assess method and relevance.

## Failure behavior

If a detail cannot be verified, write `[source detail to verify]`. If the claim cannot be supported, remove or narrow it. Never generate a plausible-looking citation from memory.
